# Style library

784 styles and prompts, each with a preview of the result. Pick one you like, copy its prompt or give its id to the plugin: `/umd:storyboard` starts from that style instead of inventing a generic look.

- **Film styles** (LemoLab): 66 complete art directions, each with a short demo film.
- **Scored motion graphics**: 15 films of 10 s scored by an AI jury (score out of 10 on the card), with the prompt that made them.
- **Creators' prompts**: 703 prompts shared with their video, sorted into explainer, motion, interactive and 3D.

In your project, the simplest is to say "use the Art Deco style" during `/umd:go`, or run `umd styles get <id>`: the prompt is downloaded into `.motion/styles/` with its credit line.
